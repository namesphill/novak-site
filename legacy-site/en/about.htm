About Us

Located in El Paso, Texas with a Manufacturing facility across the border in Chihuahua Mexico, Novak has been designing and manufacturing power conversion and microprocesor based products since 1993.

With key employees rooted in the OEM power conversion design and manufacturing industry, Novak is better able to design and deliver products that solve custom applications in a manufacturing enviroment.

Novak is dedicated to providing industrial custom and standard power supplies with features specific for the Electrochemical, Electromachining, MEMS (Nanotechnology), Semiconductor and Specialty Pulse Power areas. Novak also provides design and manufacturing private label services.

Novak has consistently provided innovative solutions and remains commited to continual improvement, product development and service to its customers and end product users.
