ABOUT US

Located in Chihuahua, Mexico, Novak has been designing and manufacturing power conversion and microprocessor-based products since 1993.

With key employees rooted in the OEM power conversion design and manufacturing industry, Novak is dedicated to providing industrial custom and standard power supplies with features specific to the electrochemical, electromachining, MEMS (nanotechnology), semiconductor, and specialty pulse power areas. Novak also offers design and manufacturing private label services.

Novak has consistently provided innovative solutions and remains committed to continual improvement, product development, and service to its customers and to the end users of its products.
