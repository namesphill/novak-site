PPa Series

DC pulse rectifiers with reverse pulses and wave sequencing. 25 – 2,000 watts

Our PPa series consists of compact, high-performance industrial power supplies designed to optimize space and reduce operating costs.

Its sealed, corrosion-resistant enclosure guarantees reliable operation even in demanding industrial environments. It offers adjustable voltage and current output, high efficiency, programmable start ramps, and a configurable manual process of up to four steps for precise process control.

Our specialty is PPa power supplies from 10A to 2,000A, with optional RS485 digital interface with optical isolation, amp-hour controller, remote control, and Ethernet — tailored to customer needs.

General characteristics

  • Sealed enclosure for harsh industrial environments
  • High efficiency, compact and lightweight
  • Soft-switching with phase control
  • Precise power control
  • Friendly user interface — menu-driven alphanumeric display
  • Automation interfaces — digital/analog for PLC or computer
  • Microprocessor-controlled operation
  • Full voltage and amperage control
  • Line (VAC) and load regulation
  • Amp-hour totalizer, programmable timers, alarms
  • Copper bus bars for output connections
  • 2-year warranty
